Do my friends need an account?

No. Send them the room link or the room code. They pick a display name and they're in.

Is Bumble Series free?

Yes. Creating rooms, chat and public rooms are free. Free rooms play through the Windows app. Bumble Series Pro is $9.99 a month and adds browser playback, larger groups and unlimited rooms.

Can I keep my room private?

Private rooms never show up in Discover. Only people with your link or code can join.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Windows 10 and 11 are available now. macOS and Linux builds are coming for Pro members first.

Someone is being a problem in my room. What do I do?

Check the room rules, then report it through Support with the room code.
